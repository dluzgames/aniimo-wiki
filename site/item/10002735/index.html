Gachapon
Each basic attack and skill triggers the gacha once, randomly firing an orange, purple, blue, or gold bubble and adding that bubble color to the gacha slots. Gold bubbles have a 1% chance to appear, while each other color has a 33% chance to appear. When the slots hold 5 bubbles of the same color, or when a gold bubble appears, all bubbles are cleared and [Jackpot] begins for 10s. In [Jackpot], basic attacks and skills no longer trigger the gacha and always fire Rainbow Bubbles. Basic attack damage +60%.
